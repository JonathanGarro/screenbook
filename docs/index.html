Insights

See how your taste compares.

Every movie you’ve rated, plotted against critics or the IMDb crowd. On the line you agree. Below it you liked it more, above it less. Hover a dot to see which film it is.

Ask Claude

Never wonder what to watch.

Ask what to watch tonight and get four picks grounded in your own ratings, checked against the streaming services you pay for, each with a line on why it fits. Add one with a click, or ask for four more.

Optional. Ask Claude uses your own Anthropic API key, so it stays off until you add one, and Anthropic bills it directly, usually a few cents a chat. What Claude reads is sent to Anthropic under that key; chats are saved only on your Mac.

Up and running in minutes.

Screenbook needs macOS 14 or later.

1. Download

Open the disk image and drag Screenbook to Applications. It’s signed and notarized by Apple, and keeps itself up to date.

2. Add your key

Get a free key at themoviedb.org, under Settings, API, and paste it into Screenbook’s Settings.

3. Point at your vault

Optional: choose your Obsidian vault and folders, and import the reviews you’ve already written.

4. Add titles

Press Cmd+N, search, and add. For a show you’re up to date on, tick “I’m caught up.”
